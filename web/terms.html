1. What the Service is — and what it isn't

The Wi-Fi Sign Generator is a free, browser-based tool that turns a Wi-Fi network name and password into a printable sign containing a QR code. When a guest scans the code with their phone's camera, the phone offers to join the network.

The Service runs entirely on your device. The web page delivers a small program (compiled to WebAssembly) to your browser, and that program does all of the work locally. The network name, password, logo, colors, and text you enter are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no server-side processing, and no storage of what you enter.
